## What we collect

**When you place an order** we collect your name, billing and delivery address, email address, phone number, what you bought and any delivery notes you add. Card payments are taken by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and we never see or store your full card number. If you pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay, the same applies.

**When you create an account** we keep your login email, a securely hashed password, your saved addresses, your order history and any wishlist you build.

**When you confirm your age.** We sell alcohol, so the site asks you to confirm that you are of legal drinking age before you enter. We store your answer in your browser so you are not asked on every page. We do not ask for or keep your date of birth.

**When you contact us** by email, phone or social media, we keep the message and your contact details so we can reply and keep a record of what was agreed.

**When you leave a review** we publish the name you give and your review. Your email address is collected with it but is not shown.

**When you join our mailing list** we collect your email address, and your name if you give it.

**When you browse the site** we collect technical information through cookies and similar tools: your IP address, device and browser type, the pages you view, how you arrived at the site and roughly where you are (city level, from your IP address). More on this under Cookies below.

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

- **To take, deliver and support your order.** This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
- **To check that alcohol is only sold to adults.** We are legally required to do this under the Licensing Act 2003. We or our courier may ask for proof of age on delivery.
- **To keep business and tax records.** This is a legal obligation.
- **To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.** We have a legitimate interest in protecting our customers and our business.
- **To answer your questions.** We have a legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us.
- **To send you marketing emails.** We only do this with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing us.
- **To measure how the site is used and how our adverts perform.** We do this with your consent, given through the cookie banner.

We do not make decisions about you by automated means, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

## Who we share it with

We share only what each of these needs to do its job:

- **Stripe**, which processes payments.
- **Our delivery companies**, which receive your name, address, phone number and any delivery notes so they can bring your order to you.
- **Mailchimp**, which stores our mailing list and sends our emails.
- **Google**, for Google Analytics (site statistics) and Google Ads (measuring whether our adverts lead to orders).
- **Meta**, for the Meta pixel, which measures how our Facebook and Instagram adverts perform.
- **Our website hosting company and our web developers**, who may come across personal information while running and maintaining the site.
- **Our accountants, HMRC, the police or a court**, where the law requires it.

Some of these companies are based in, or store information in, countries outside the UK, including the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised in UK law, such as the UK extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- **Orders and invoices:** six years after the end of the financial year they belong to, because tax law requires it.
- **Your account:** until you ask us to close it. Records of past orders are kept for the six years above.
- **Emails and other messages:** up to two years after the conversation ends, unless they relate to an order.
- **Mailing list:** until you unsubscribe.
- **Analytics information:** no longer than 14 months.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser.

**Cookies the shop cannot work without.** These remember what is in your basket, keep you logged in, remember that you have confirmed your age and record your cookie choices. They do not need your consent.

**Statistics cookies (Google Analytics).** These tell us which pages are visited and how people find the site. We only set them if you accept them in the cookie banner.

**Advertising cookies (Google Ads and Meta).** These tell us whether someone who saw one of our adverts went on to visit or buy. We only set them if you accept them in the cookie banner.

You can change your mind at any time by reopening the cookie banner, and you can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the necessary cookies, the basket and checkout will not work.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
- ask us to correct anything that is wrong
- ask us to delete your information, where we have no legal reason to keep it
- ask us to stop or limit how we use it
- object to us using it on the basis of our legitimate interests
- withdraw your consent to marketing or to cookies at any time
- ask for your information in a format you can take to another company

To use any of these rights, email info@sidwells.net. We will reply within one month and we do not charge for this.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
